Privacy Policy

Updated October 27, 2026

Overview

This policy describes Drag Race Sim and the optional rewarded advertising being introduced in version 1.2.1. Version 1.2 and earlier do not include third-party advertising. We do not operate a server that receives gameplay saves or sell those saves.

Game Data

Driver profiles, garage contents, tuning setups, race results, and career progress are stored on the player's device. If the player turns on Sync progress with iCloud in Settings, a copy is saved in their private iCloud database (CloudKit) so progress can sync between devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Earlier versions synced through iCloud key-value storage; the app still reads a save left there so it can be moved over. Nerdquad does not operate a server that receives or reads this game data.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. When the player signs in, authentication and their Game Center profile are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and the player's Game Center settings. The app does not submit scores or achievements.

Optional Rewarded Advertising (Version 1.2.1)

You may choose to watch a Google AdMob ad for a stated reward: extra virtual game cash or one second attempt at a lost race. A retry can change the official race and championship result, has no additional entry fee, and pays winnings once. Virtual currency has no real-world cash value. You can decline and keep playing. No banner ads or app-open ads are used, and ads do not appear automatically during racing.

Advertising Data and Diagnostics

When you choose an ad, Google's Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform SDKs may process IP address (including approximate location inferred from it), app- or device-related identifiers, ad views and interactions, product interactions, crash information and performance diagnostics. Google uses this information for advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Gameplay saves are not provided to AdMob by the app.

The app requests non-personalized ads, does not request Apple's tracking permission and does not access IDFA with tracking authorization. Non-personalized ads still involve advertising and diagnostic data processing. Apple may separately provide aggregated App Store and diagnostic reports according to your device settings.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps.

Your Ad Privacy Choices

Where required, Google's consent message is shown before an ad request. Required privacy controls can be reopened with Ad privacy choices in Settings. After you have chosen an ad, opening Settings asks Google's consent service whether those controls apply to you; this check does not request an ad. You may decline advertising and continue racing. Ad availability depends on your connection, consent eligibility and Google's ad supply. Google controls retention of the advertising information it processes as described in its policies.

Children

Drag Race Sim is a general-audience racing game. The developer does not ask children to provide personal information. Advertising is limited to general-rated content, but Google's SDK data practices described above still apply when ads are used. Parents can contact us with privacy questions.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
