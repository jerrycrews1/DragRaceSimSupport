Support

Help for Drag Race Sim on iPhone.

Progress and iCloud

Progress saves automatically. To sync across devices, sign in to the same Apple Account and enable iCloud Drive. Open Settings inside the game and use Sync Now to request an immediate refresh.

Game Center

Leaderboards and achievements require Game Center. Confirm that Game Center is enabled in iOS Settings, then reopen the game.

Resetting a Driver

Open Settings and choose Reset Driver Data. The game shows a final confirmation before deleting the active driver, cars, parts, statistics, and career progress.

Race Controls

Race scenes run in landscape. Outside a race, the interface follows the device orientation. If controls appear misaligned after rotating, return to the previous screen and reopen the race.

Optional Rewards in Version 1.2.1

The upcoming update offers a choice to watch an ad for $250 in virtual game cash or one real retry after a completed race loss. You can always decline and continue racing. Version 1.2 and earlier have no third-party ads.

A rewarded retry uses the same opponent and conditions, with no extra entry fee. The second attempt replaces the first official result and winnings are awarded once. It is available once per event, including championships. If you leave or close the app during the retry, the original loss stands and the retry stays used.

An Ad or Reward Is Unavailable

Ads require an internet connection, eligible consent settings and available ad supply. If no ad is available, keep playing and try later. A reward is unlocked only when Google confirms it was earned; closing an ad early does not grant it. Resolve any save warning before trying again. If a cash bonus could not save, collect it from Home without watching another ad while that session remains open.

Where required, use Ad privacy choices in Settings to review your consent choices. See the privacy policy for advertising data details.
